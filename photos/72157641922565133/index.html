Photo album: The Tongariro Crossing

The Tongariro Crossing is said by many to be the best day walk in NZ and maybe even the world... 20 kilometers across a crazy moonscape between volcanoes and lakes. And a side trip up Mount Doom aka Mount Ngauruhoe!
